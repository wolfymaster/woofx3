# woofx3

The basics every stream needs, built in and ready to use: chat commands, alerts, counters, timers, queues, schedules, forms, databases and the widgets that show them on your overlay.

## What you can do

- Reply to chat: send a message, post an announcement or pin a message, on one platform or all of them
- Run a chat command's steps from anywhere, or react when a viewer uses a command
- Play an alert on stream with text, images, video, sound and animations
- Show or hide a widget on a scene
- Count things: deaths, wins, hugs. Add to it, take away, set or reset it, and cheer when it reaches a goal
- Run a countdown for breaks, subathons or giveaways. Start, pause, add time and react when it ends
- Keep a line of viewers or song requests. Add, take the next one, remove, clear, or move entries to another queue
- Do something on a repeat, like a chat message every 15 minutes, only while live or all the time
- Ask viewers questions on your public page and use their answers
- Keep a table of rows, such as form answers or giveaway entrants, and show a value from it on stream
- Look up a viewer: who they are, their avatar and whether they are live
- React when a viewer joins or leaves a group of viewers you defined
- React when a module is installed, or when a workflow is created, changed or deleted

## Widgets for your scenes

- Alert: where your alerts play
- Text, Image, Video, Audio and Lottie animation: the pieces of an alert, or plain decoration on a scene
- Counter, Timer and Queue: live numbers, countdowns and lines on your overlay
- Database value: one value from a database row
